# Product information and research register

Date: 2026-10-17

## Evidence model

The public catalogue is limited to product identity, category, supplied product image, concise product description and common use context. It does not publish dimensions, grades, standards, pressure ratings, compositions, availability, prices, warranties or manufacturing claims unless Kanan supplies an exact supporting source.

## Approved Kanan catalogue evidence

All 28 published products are supported by the named source images supplied for this project in `kanan-products` and mapped in `data/products.ts`:

- Scrap (3): Heavy Melting Scrap (HMS), Cast Iron Scrap, Scrap Metal.
- Steel (10): TMT B500 Steel Bars, TMT B300 Steel Bars, Hollow Section, Wire Rod, Black Pipes, Angle Iron, Flat Bars, HR Plates, Z-Purlins and Chequered Plates.
- Roofing (5): Aluzinki Corrugated Sheets, Rangi Corrugated Sheets, IT4 and IT5 Roofing Sheets, Wavestyle Roofing Sheets and Mountstyle Roofing Sheets.
- Cement (3): Dangote Cement 42.5N, Dangote Cement 42.5R and Huaxin Cement.
- Plastic (7): Recycled PP Pellets, Recycled PE Pellets, TriTank Water Tanks, UPVC Borewell Pipes, UPVC Pipes, HDPE Pipes and PPR Pipes.

## External research

- Lodhia's public roofing catalogue and matching individual IT4/IT5 and Rangi pages were reviewed on 2026-10-17. They corroborate the existence of the matching roofing names and category terminology only. Lodhia's performance, material, colour, warranty and manufacturer claims are not reproduced because the repository contains no source that ties Kanan's exact variants to those claims.
- Kamal Steel's public Kamal 500 page was reviewed as an information-architecture reference only. Its technical figures, standards, certifications and manufacturing claims do not apply to Kanan's TMT B500 listing and are not used.

## Verification needed from Kanan

For every listed product, obtain the exact manufacturer/brand (where applicable), product data sheet, grade/profile/size/variant information and written permission or supplier relationship confirmation before publishing technical specifications or manufacturer claims. In particular, confirm whether any Lodhia product information applies to the exact Aluzinki, Rangi, IT4/IT5, Wavestyle and Mountstyle variants Kanan supplies.
